## Gaps (nothing below is written in the data, because no official text could be downloaded or it was out of the task)

- France, loi n° 94-665 du 4 août 1994, art. 2 (French language for instructions): NOT OBTAINED. www.legifrance.gouv.fr answers HTTP 403 (browser challenge) to scripted requests; the DILA open data server (echanges.dila.gouv.fr/OPENDATA/LEGI) resets the connection; the PISTE Légifrance API needs credentials that are not on this machine.
- France, Code de l'environnement, art. L541-9-3 (sorting information, Triman), L541-10-13 (unique identifier) and related EPR provisions: NOT OBTAINED, same three failures. Consequence: WEEE registration, eco-organisation membership, Triman and the unique identifier are not sourced for France; only the Directive (addressed to Member States) is quoted.
- French transposition of the LVD, EMC, RoHS and radio equipment Directives (the texts that bind the seller in France): not downloaded. The entries quote the Directives.
- Regulation (EC) No 765/2008, Art. 30 (general principles of the CE marking), to which LVD Art. 16, EMC Art. 16 and RoHS Art. 14 refer: not extracted.
- Regulation (EU) 2019/1782: Annex II tables are flattened to reading order (row by row); the nameplate and website tables are quoted only by their introductory sentence; Annex III (verification tolerances) not extracted. A corrigendum cannot be ruled out because the consolidated version is not available in English from the Publications Office.
- Harmonised standards (for instance the safety and EMC standards usually cited in a declaration of conformity) are not law texts and are not listed: none was downloaded.
- Not examined: REACH (Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006), POPs, the Packaging Regulation (EU) 2025/40 for the sales packaging, the Cyber Resilience Act, energy labelling, customs formalities.
- Article 4(5) of Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 is quoted with the footnote reference numbers of the consolidated text, shown as "( 4 )", "( 5 )" and so on.
